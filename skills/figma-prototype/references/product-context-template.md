Decisions are not kept here: they go to the context document's §6, the project's one decision log, with Scope `Product`. A line with no source is `[TBD]`. Keep it under 200 lines; past that, this page becomes an overview that links a file per subject.

For a suite of products, keep to what its apps share: the shell, its words, its roles. How one app works (its records, its settings, its own flows) is that app's, not this file's; *Flows* below holds only the flows that cross the suite, such as its navigation.

What it is not: the library's conventions, settings and rulings are the context document's (`design-system-context.md`, beside this file); general design guidance is the toolkit's `knowledge/`; what a screen contains is the stored screens'.
